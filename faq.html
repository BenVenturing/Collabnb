Frequently asked questions

Collabnb is a two-sided marketplace that connects content creators — UGC filmmakers, micro-influencers, travel creators — with boutique hospitality hosts. Creators apply for stays in exchange for content deliverables. Hosts post collab listings to find and vet creators who'll tell their story. It's not an influencer agency and it's not a travel booking platform. It's direct collab infrastructure for both sides of the deal.

Airbnb is for paying guests. Collabnb is for collab arrangements where the stay is exchanged for content — there's no money changing hands for the room itself, just an agreement on deliverables. Unlike Influencer platforms, we're not an agency taking a cut, we don't offer gifting campaigns for mass brands, and we don't cater to large hotel chains. We focus exclusively on the boutique end of hospitality and the creator-first end of travel content.

The first 100 verified creators and the first 100 verified hosts — 200 total founding members — will receive lifetime access to every Collabnb feature, regardless of any future pricing we introduce. "Verified" means our team has reviewed your application and approved it (manual audit, not automated). We track by submission date, so signing up early matters. Access is per-role: 100 creator spots and 100 host spots are separate pools.

Short-form vertical video is the most common ask — TikToks, Instagram Reels. Some hosts want UGC-style (no face, raw footage they can license for ads). Others want lifestyle content for their own social channels. A few want written content or photography alongside video. Each host sets their own brief when they list a stay, so you'll always know exactly what's expected before you apply.

Explore is personalized around three signals. Location — listings near your profile city and region rank first, followed by places you've already completed collabs. Content match — your chosen content niches (set in Edit Profile) plus the vibe of listings you've saved and worked with are compared against each listing's tags; strong fits show a "% match" badge. Profile fit — whether the host's requested creator tier and content type line up with yours. Nothing is ever hidden: every published listing stays visible in "All Stays" and through search — the signals only decide what gets surfaced first. Searching by destination, content type, or dates always overrides personalization.

Privacy Policy

Introduction

We automatically collect usage data including device information, IP address, and interactions with the platform to improve performance and user experience.

How We Use Information

Your information is used to operate and improve Collabnb, including enabling collaborations, displaying profiles and listings, processing payments, and facilitating communication between users.

We may also use your data to send updates, ensure platform security, prevent fraud, and enhance the overall user experience.

Sharing of Information

Your information is shared with other users as necessary to enable collaborations, including displaying profiles, listings, and communication between relevant parties.

We may also share data with trusted third-party service providers or when required by law. We do not sell your personal data.

By using the platform, you agree to applicable fees and payment structures as outlined within the app.

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, and maintain records. You may request account deletion at any time.

We implement reasonable safeguards to protect your data, but no system is completely secure. Use of the platform is at your own risk.

As a global platform, your data may be transferred and processed internationally. By using Collabnb, you consent to this.

Platform Administration

Collabnb is operated by a platform administrator who has access to certain account and activity data in order to maintain a safe, functioning marketplace. Specifically, the administrator may:

  • View user profiles, account status, and verification details
  • Review and manage collaboration records between creators and hosts

This access is used solely for operating and improving the platform — not for commercial resale of your data. Admin activity is logged and subject to the same data protection standards outlined in this policy.
