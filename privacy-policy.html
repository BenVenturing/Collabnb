Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 15, 2026

Introduction

Collabnb ("we," "our," or "us") connects content creators with hospitality hosts for collaboration opportunities. We are committed to protecting your personal data and being transparent about how it is collected and used.

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the legal bases we rely on to process it (see the "Notice to European Users" section below for details). If you do not agree with this policy, please discontinue use of the platform.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information such as your name, email, profile details, social media handles, and property or listing information depending on your role. We also collect content you upload, collaboration details, and messages exchanged on the platform.

We automatically collect usage data including device information, IP address, and interactions with the platform to improve performance and user experience. See our Cookie Policy for the specific cookies and storage involved.

If you grant permission, we also collect your device's precise location — for example to suggest nearby destinations on Explore or to auto-fill your city on your profile. This only happens with your consent via your browser's location permission, and the coordinates are processed through third-party mapping and reverse-geocoding services (see below).

Please don't submit sensitive personal information to us (for example government ID numbers, health information, or financial account details) unless we specifically request it as part of identity or payout verification.

How We Use Information

Your information is used to operate and improve Collabnb, including enabling collaborations, displaying profiles and listings, processing payments, and facilitating communication between users.

We may also use your data to send updates, ensure platform security, prevent fraud, and enhance the overall user experience.

We may use your information to suggest relevant listings or collaborators to you — for example through Stay Alerts. This helps surface matches, but it is never used to make a decision with legal or similarly significant effects on you (such as denying you access to the platform), and does not involve that kind of automated profiling.

Sharing of Information

Your information is shared with other users as necessary to enable collaborations, including displaying profiles, listings, and communication between relevant parties.

We may also share data with trusted third-party service providers or when required by law. These providers include our authentication provider (Clerk), database and backend infrastructure (Convex), payment processor (Stripe), mapping and location services (Mapbox and OpenStreetMap), and hosting infrastructure (Cloudflare) — each receives only the data needed to perform its function. We do not sell your personal data.

Notifications & Digital Wallet Passes

If you choose to connect a Google Wallet pass (Settings > Notifications), we share limited information with Google to create and update that pass: your name, your role (host or creator), your Collabnb membership start date, and a referral link unique to your account. Google's own handling of this data is governed by its privacy policy.

We use this connection only to push account-related alerts — new messages, application activity, and reminders — to your device. It is never used for advertising, and no data beyond what's listed above is shared for this feature. You can disconnect it at any time from Settings > Notifications or by removing the pass from your Google Wallet app.

Payments

Payments for listing fees or collaborations are processed through third-party providers. Collabnb does not store full payment details. See our Refund Policy for how fees and payments are handled.

By using the platform, you agree to applicable fees and payment structures as outlined within the app.

Data Retention & Rights

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, and maintain records. How long depends on the type of data, why we collected it, and whether we're required to keep it for legal, accounting, or safety reasons — data tied to an active account is generally kept for as long as the account exists. You may request account deletion at any time.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or port your data, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it — see the European and U.S. state-specific sections below for the full detail. Contact us at hellocollabnb@gmail.com.

You can opt out of marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in those emails, or from Settings > Notifications; this won't affect service-related emails like messages or contract updates. We don't currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, though we do honor the Global Privacy Control signal as an opt-out request where applicable (see the State Privacy Rights section below).

Security & International Use

We implement reasonable safeguards to protect your data, but no system is completely secure. Use of the platform is at your own risk.

As a global platform, your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where we transfer personal data out of the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we rely on an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. See the "Notice to European Users" section below for more detail.

Children

Collabnb is not intended for use by anyone under 18 years of age, and account creation requires confirming you are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information in violation of this policy, please contact us at hellocollabnb@gmail.com so we can delete it.

Notice to European Users

This section applies to individuals in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland ("Europe"), and supplements the rest of this Privacy Policy.

Controller. Collabnb is the controller of your personal information for purposes of the GDPR and UK GDPR. See the Updates & Contact section below for how to reach us.

Our legal bases for processing. For each purpose described in "How We Use Information" above, we rely on one of the following:

  • Contractual necessity — creating your account, operating collaborations, displaying profiles and listings, processing payments, and enabling messaging between users.
  • Legitimate interests — platform security and fraud prevention, service improvement and analytics, surfacing relevant listings or collaborators (e.g. Stay Alerts), and maintaining admin audit logs.
  • Compliance with law — responding to legal process, tax and accounting obligations, and enforcing our Terms of Service.
  • Consent — marketing emails, connecting a Google Wallet pass, and using your device's precise location.

Your rights. If you are in Europe, you can ask us to give you access to your personal information, correct it, delete it, restrict how we process it, object to processing based on legitimate interests, transfer a machine-readable copy to you or a third party, or — where we rely on consent — withdraw it at any time. To exercise any of these, email hellocollabnb@gmail.com; we may ask you to verify your identity first. If you're not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority — in the UK, this is the Information Commissioner's Office.

International transfers. We are a U.S.-based company, so using Collabnb means your personal information is processed in the United States. Where we or our service providers transfer personal information out of Europe, we rely on an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your U.S. State Privacy Rights

If you are a resident of a U.S. state with its own privacy law (such as California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have some or all of the rights below, subject to that state's specific law and its exceptions: to know or access the categories of personal information we've collected and why, to delete it, to correct inaccuracies, to opt out of its sale or sharing, to non-discrimination for exercising these rights, and to appeal a denied request.

Categories of personal information we collect. In the preceding 12 months we have collected: identifiers (name, email, username); commercial information (subscription tier, payment status — not full card numbers, which our payment processor holds); internet and device activity (device data, IP address, usage patterns); precise geolocation (only with your permission); professional information (business name, for hosts); and user-generated content (listings, messages, profile content).

We do not sell any of these categories for money, and we do not share them for cross-context behavioral advertising. We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) as a valid opt-out signal where required by law. We do not have actual knowledge that we collect, sell, or share personal information belonging to anyone under 16.

You can exercise any of these rights, or designate an authorized agent to do so on your behalf, by emailing hellocollabnb@gmail.com; we may need to verify your identity first. California residents may also request, once a year, information about our disclosure of personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing purposes by emailing us with "Shine the Light Request" in the subject line.

Platform Administration

Collabnb is operated by a platform administrator who has access to certain account and activity data in order to maintain a safe, functioning marketplace. Specifically, the administrator may:

  • View user profiles, account status, and verification details
  • Review and manage collaboration records between creators and hosts
  • View, feature, or remove platform listings
  • Moderate user-submitted suggestions and feedback
  • Access platform-wide analytics (aggregate usage, sign-up data)
  • Send broadcast messages or platform-wide announcements
  • Review audit logs of key platform actions for security purposes

This access is used solely for operating and improving the platform — not for commercial resale of your data. Admin activity is logged and subject to the same data protection standards outlined in this policy.

Updates & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the platform indicates acceptance of any changes.
