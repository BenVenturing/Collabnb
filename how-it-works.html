Become a Founder
From signup to first collab in three steps.
Creator track

For Creators

Get verified, browse listings, and start collaborating. Each listing sets its own terms — hosted stays, cash compensation, or hybrid partnerships.

1

Apply to the waitlist

Tell us your TikTok and Instagram handles, your creator tier, and how many collabs you've done in the last three months. It takes about two minutes. No lengthy applications, no agency gatekeeping.

UGC Beginner UGC Pro Micro Influencer
2

Get verified

Our team does a manual review of every creator — checking your content, your niche, and your fit for boutique hospitality collabs. This is how we keep quality high on both sides. The first 200 verified creators get lifetime access to Collabnb at launch.

3

Browse, apply, create, travel

Once the app launches July 15, you’ll get access to a discovery feed of boutique stays actively hiring creators for professional campaigns. Each listing shows the compensation — cash fee, hosted stay, or hybrid — plus deliverables, timeline, and creative brief. Browse by location, property type, or deliverable style. Apply directly in the app, and go create.

Host track

For Hosts

Post a stay, set your terms, and get matched with the right creator for your brand.

1

Sign up as a host

Tell us about your property — the type, location, and what makes it interesting. We work with boutique hotels, BnBs, glamping sites, small resorts, curated experiences, and tourism boards. If you care about storytelling, you're a fit.

Boutique Hotel BnB Glamping Small Resort Experience
2

List your first collab stay

When the app launches, you’ll post a campaign: the stay details, the creative brief (e.g. 3 Reels + 1 TikTok), the length of stay, and compensation type — cash fee, hosted stay, or hybrid. You set the terms. Vetted creators apply to you — not the other way around.

3

Review, approve, host

Review creator applications in the app — their portfolio, tier, past collabs, and fit for your property. Approve the best match, confirm the dates, and host them. After the stay, receive your deliverables. No agencies, no awkward cold outreach.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 15, 2026

Introduction

Collabnb ("we," "our," or "us") connects content creators with hospitality hosts for collaboration opportunities. We are committed to protecting your personal data and being transparent about how it is collected and used.

By using Collabnb, you agree to the collection and use of your information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the platform.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information such as your name, email, profile details, social media handles, and property or listing information depending on your role. We also collect content you upload, collaboration details, and messages exchanged on the platform.

We automatically collect usage data including device information, IP address, and interactions with the platform to improve performance and user experience.

How We Use Information

Your information is used to operate and improve Collabnb, including enabling collaborations, displaying profiles and listings, processing payments, and facilitating communication between users.

We may also use your data to send updates, ensure platform security, prevent fraud, and enhance the overall user experience.

Sharing of Information

Your information is shared with other users as necessary to enable collaborations, including displaying profiles, listings, and communication between relevant parties.

We may also share data with trusted third-party service providers or when required by law. We do not sell your personal data.

Payments

Payments for listing fees or collaborations are processed through third-party providers. Collabnb does not store full payment details.

By using the platform, you agree to applicable fees and payment structures as outlined within the app.

Data Retention & Rights

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, and maintain records. You may request account deletion at any time.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, update, or delete your data. Contact us at hellocollabnb@gmail.com.

Security & International Use

We implement reasonable safeguards to protect your data, but no system is completely secure. Use of the platform is at your own risk.

As a global platform, your data may be transferred and processed internationally. By using Collabnb, you consent to this.

Platform Administration

Collabnb is operated by a platform administrator who has access to certain account and activity data in order to maintain a safe, functioning marketplace. Specifically, the administrator may:

  • View user profiles, account status, and verification details
  • Review and manage collaboration records between creators and hosts
  • View, feature, or remove platform listings
  • Moderate user-submitted suggestions and feedback
  • Access platform-wide analytics (aggregate usage, sign-up data)
  • Send broadcast messages or platform-wide announcements
  • Review audit logs of key platform actions for security purposes

This access is used solely for operating and improving the platform — not for commercial resale of your data. Admin activity is logged and subject to the same data protection standards outlined in this policy.
